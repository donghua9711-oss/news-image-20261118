# The day's stories, and how each was verified (18 November 2026)

The image is a one-page newspaper, "The World in One Day", summing up
Wednesday 18 November 2026 in 29 stories: a lead story, eight
illustrated stories, and twenty one-line items, under a band of six
headline numbers. Every story happened, or was first reported, on
Wednesday 18 November 2026. Stories were cross-checked across at
least two independent roundups of the day where possible; the few
that rest on a single roundup are marked as such below, and disputed
claims are attributed on the page itself, not stated as fact.

## The six headline numbers

- **141** — Russian drones over Ukraine in one night, 126 of them
  downed or suppressed (Ukrainian Air Force figures, widely carried).
- **950,000** — displaced Palestinians on flood-prone ground in Gaza
  entering a fourth winter (WHO, via UN News and Al Jazeera).
- **204,070** — people identified for evacuation in eight Odisha
  districts ahead of Cyclone Akvan (Odisha Special Relief
  Commissioner via IANS).
- **56-44** — Democrats' projected majority in the incoming US
  Senate after the Alaska result (AP).
- **$115.8bn** — Nvidia's record quarterly revenue (company results,
  carried by the day's market wraps).
- **1 light-day** — Voyager 1's distance from Earth at 05:16 EST on
  18 November, the first human-made object to reach it (NASA).

## The illustrated stories (1-9)

1. **APEC, Shenzhen (lead, cover illustration).** Xi Jinping met
   Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Wednesday 18 November; Donald Trump
   arrived and was greeted by Xi, and a Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral was
   set for Thursday 19 November (AFP and AP, via Open Chronicle's
   18 November global brief and the day's US strategic briefs).
   Indonesia's President Prabowo also attended the summit.
2. **Iran and the Gulf.** Iran targeted US installations at Kuwait's
   Ali Al Salem base and in Bahrain before dawn, after US jets struck
   an Iranian missile launch site on Qeshm Island; shipping through
   the Strait of Hormuz stayed disrupted (Reuters reporting carried
   by US Brief, Al Jazeera, and the day's video roundups).
3. **Kerch Bridge.** Ukraine's military said a second straight night
   of strikes collapsed part of the road span of the bridge to
   Crimea; Russia confirmed damage and said it would rebuild, with
   trains running and cars sent to ferries (US Brief, 18 November;
   claim and counter-claim both carried in the day's roundups).
4. **Gaza.** The US-backed Board of Peace warned the UN that the
   Gaza ceasefire could collapse, faulting both Israel and Hamas
   (Al Jazeera, 18 November). UN agencies put the displaced at more
   than 1.7 million, with winter cold and flooding the immediate
   threat (UN News, WFP, IOM figures in the day's roundups).
5. **Cyclone Akvan.** The India Meteorological Department's Wednesday
   bulletin put the severe cyclone ashore on Friday 20 November
   between Narsapur and Puri, winds 90-100 km/h gusting to 110.
   Odisha's chief minister chaired the preparedness review on
   Wednesday; over 47,000 people had already been moved and 74
   rescue teams deployed (IMD via IANS, The Hindu BusinessLine and
   Nation Press, 18 November).
6. **US Senate.** Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican
   incumbent Dan Sullivan in Alaska, becoming the first Alaska
   Native senator and giving Democrats a projected 56-44 majority
   in the incoming Senate (AP and Alaska Public Media, via Open
   Chronicle).
7. **Nvidia.** Quarterly revenue doubled to a record $115.8bn,
   beating expectations, while the board ruled out reinstating
   founder Jensen Huang as chief executive (company results and
   board reporting in the day's market and tech roundups: PSG
   Wealth daily highlights, Investopedia, Medium's strategic brief).
8. **Ebola, DR Congo.** The outbreak passed 11,000 cases on
   Wednesday (single source: the Daily News 18 November world
   roundup; no contradicting figure found in the day's other
   roundups).
9. **A stellar merger in Andromeda.** NASA's 18 November release:
   Webb data clinches a first-of-its-kind merger - a star slightly
   less massive than the Sun spiralled into a swollen supergiant in
   the Andromeda galaxy; the merged star is nicknamed Bao (NASA;
   Nature study via Daily Inference, 18 November). A rival model
   exists; the page follows NASA's own headline.

## Also on Wednesday (10-29)

10. **COP31, Antalya.** Negotiators entered the final 48 hours
    working through a draft with 144 disputed items; the summit was
    scheduled to conclude Friday 20 November (the day's headlines
    roundup; Open Chronicle also flags the talks).
11. **Anthropic IPO.** Anthropic was to finalize IPO pricing on
    Wednesday evening, with demand reported likely to top the range
    (Wall Street Journal via Investopedia; Edge Consultancy market
    wrap).
12. **OpenAI.** GPT-6.2 "Astra" and "Sol" launched with lower API
    prices (single source: Daily News tech roundup, 18 November).
13. **POW swap.** Russia and Ukraine agreed to their largest
    prisoner swap yet (single source: US Brief headline, 18 Nov).
14. **ASEAN and Myanmar.** ASEAN set out terms for Myanmar's return
    (single source: US Brief headline, 18 November).
15. **Knesset speaker.** Israel's parliament elected Matan Kahana
    as speaker, 66-52 (Daily News roundup; GeoWarp roundup gives
    the same vote).
16. **Israel's government.** President Herzog asked Gadi Eisenkot
    to form a government (single source: US Brief, 18 November).
17. **Turkey.** Authorities took control of an opposition
    broadcaster (single source: Medium's strategic brief, 18 Nov).
18. **Indonesia-China.** Four strategic partnerships worth $71.8bn,
    including ten smart cities (single source: GeoWarp roundup).
19. **UK inflation.** October inflation rose to 3.6%, a one-year
    high, on fuel and energy costs (single source: GeoWarp).
20. **Fed minutes.** The October minutes showed nearly all
    officials expecting further rate rises; the dollar hit a
    19-month high (The Motley Fool, 18 November).
21. **Markets close.** The Dow fell 1.11% to 50,198, a third
    straight loss; the S&P 500 slipped 0.23% while the Nasdaq rose
    0.42%; gold fell 3.51% to $3,728.60 (Edge Consultancy US Market
    Wrap, 18 November; The Motley Fool).
22. **Carney at APEC.** Canada's Mark Carney met Trump on the APEC
    sidelines in Shenzhen (Daily News roundup; Canadian PMO readout
    listed among its sources).
23. **Riyadh.** Debris from an intercepted attack damaged an Aramco
    tower in Riyadh (single source: US Brief headline, 18 Nov).
24. **Measles.** The United States lost its measles-free status
    after 26 years (single source: Daily News roundup, 18 Nov).
25. **James Bond.** Robert Pattinson was named the new James Bond
    (single source: the day's headlines roundup, 18 November).
26. **Ancient ice.** NASA scientists reported that stratospheric
    dust grains point to an ancient reservoir of ice in the Kuiper
    Belt, beyond Neptune (NASA Science, 18 November).
27. **Pakistan fuel.** Petrol cut by Rs3 a litre to Rs325 under an
    OGRA notification effective Wednesday (single source: the day's
    headlines roundup, 18 November).
28. **Russian refining.** Ukraine says its drone campaign has cut
    Russian oil refining by about 45%, disrupting 15 major
    refineries - attributed on the page to Ukraine, as reported
    (GeoWarp roundup, 18 November).
29. **Voyager 1.** At 05:16:07 EST on Wednesday 18 November,
    Voyager 1 reached 16.094 billion miles from Earth - one
    light-day - 49 years after launch (NASA, reported 18 November).
